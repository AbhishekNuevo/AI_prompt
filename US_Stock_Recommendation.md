# Institutional Research System — Recommended Workflow

## Step 1 — Start a New Chat

Upload the following documents:

* `Document-1-Operating-Manual.md`
* `Document-2-Research-Workflow.md`

Then send:

```text
Read both attached documents.

Document 1 defines how you think.

Document 2 defines how you execute research.

Follow both documents strictly for the remainder of this session.

Use current information and cite sources.

Do not execute any phase yet.

Wait for instructions.
```

---

# Step 2 — Initial Screening

Send:

```text
Execute:

Phase 1
Phase 2

Return:

Top 50 ranked companies
Top 20 shortlisted companies

Do not proceed further.
```

### Objective

Generate an initial universe of high-quality candidates and identify the strongest opportunities for deeper analysis.

---

# Step 3 — Challenge the Results

Do not automatically trust the Top 20 shortlist.

Send:

```text
Using Document 1:

Apply the Disconfirming Evidence Requirement.

Attempt to prove the Top 20 selections wrong.

Identify:

- Weakest candidates
- Overvalued candidates
- Candidates with weak evidence

Remove any stock that does not survive scrutiny.
```

### Objective

Eliminate weak ideas before investing time in detailed research.

---

Execute:

```text
Execute:

Phase 13
Phase 14
Phase 15
Phase 16
Phase 17
Phase 18
Phase 19

Challenge every recommendation aggressively.

Assume capital is scarce.

Identify:

- Top 3
- Single Best Stock

```

### Objective

Force every investment thesis to survive adversarial scrutiny.

Only the highest-conviction ideas should remain.

---

# Step 7 — Pre-Investment Deep Dive

Before committing capital to any stock, perform a dedicated review.

Send:

```text
Deep dive only this stock:

[TICKER]

Perform:

- Full thesis review
- Bear case review
- Competitor challenge
- Valuation review
- Thesis break conditions

Answer:

What would make this investment fail?
```

### Objective

Understand failure scenarios before risking capital.

A successful investor studies downside risk before upside potential.

# Ongoing Portfolio Review

At the end of every month or quarter:

1. Upload both documents again.
2. Provide your current holdings.

Example:

```text
Current Holdings

NVDA
Purchase Date: 2026-06-05
Purchase Price: $185
Shares: 20

Execute:

Phase 20
Phase 20A
```

Return:

```text
- Buy %
- Hold %
- Sell %
- Would I Buy It Today?
- Thesis Change Log
- Decision Journal
```

### Objective

Convert the research framework into a continuous portfolio management system.

The goal is not merely selecting stocks, but continuously validating or invalidating the original investment thesis as new information emerges.
