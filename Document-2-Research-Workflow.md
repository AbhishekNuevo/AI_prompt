# 📊 Equity Research Execution Workflow

> **Purpose:**
> This workflow defines a structured institutional-grade equity research process for evaluating U.S.-listed companies from initial screening through portfolio construction and ongoing monitoring.

⚠️ **Important**

* Follow all rules defined in **Document 1: Institutional Equity Research Operating Manual**.
* Execute **only the phase explicitly requested by the user**.
* Do **not** automatically continue to later phases unless instructed.

---

# Phase 1 — Investable Universe Creation

## Objective

Create an investable universe of high-quality U.S.-listed companies.

### Exclusions

* Penny Stocks
* OTC Securities
* Shell Companies
* SPACs
* Illiquid Securities
* Companies with Insufficient Disclosure

### Minimum Requirements

| Requirement                  | Threshold     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------- |
| Market Capitalization        | > $5 Billion  |
| Average Daily Trading Volume | > $20 Million |
| Analyst Coverage             | Required      |
| Business Viability           | Positive      |

### Screening Criteria

* Revenue Growth
* EPS Growth
* Free Cash Flow Growth
* Balance Sheet Strength
* Profitability
* Industry Tailwinds
* Economic Moat
* Management Quality

### Output

| Ticker | Company | Sector | Market Cap | Reason Included |
| ------ | ------- | ------ | ---------- | --------------- |

### Deliverable

* Top 50 Candidate Companies
* No deep analysis at this stage

# Phase 21 — Research Quality Assessment

## Evidence Quality Grades

| Grade | Meaning           |
| ----- | ----------------- |
| A     | Strong Evidence   |
| B     | Good Evidence     |
| C     | Moderate Evidence |
| D     | Weak Evidence     |

## Assessment Factors

* Data Availability
* Forecast Visibility
* Analyst Coverage
* Industry Stability
* Confidence in Assumptions
* Consistency of Evidence

### Deliverable

* Research Quality Grade
* Supporting Reasoning
* Impact on Confidence Score

### Rules

* Low Evidence Quality → Lower Conviction
* High Uncertainty → Lower Confidence
* Weak Evidence Cannot Support High Conviction
